The v0.3 setup bundle includes extract-xiso, an Xbox ISO extraction utility by
in <in@fishtank.com>, maintained by the XboxDev organization. The included
binary is used only to extract an ISO selected locally by the user.

Upstream license (from https://github.com/xboxdev/extract-xiso/blob/master/LICENSE.TXT):

This is an xdvdfs (xbox) file creation/extraction utility.
